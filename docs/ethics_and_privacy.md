# Ethics and privacy

- Public visibility of posts on Weibo does not imply unrestricted redistribution rights.
- This repository does not publish raw post text, usernames, or account identifiers.
- Deleted and inaccessible posts are outside the survivor corpus and are not redistributed.
- Engagement outcomes describe interaction within accessible posts only; they are not measures of algorithmic exposure.
- LLM labels are fallible structured measurements. Appendix B reports pooled and wave-specific validation, including Indirect/Mixed recall = 0.500 in both waves, and a misclassification check of the 14.0% to 29.4% contrast. Corrected shares and odds ratios in that check do not replace the published estimates.
- Row-level validation records are not published. Public validation materials are aggregate rates, shares, and odds ratios only.
- Test fixtures use synthetic placeholders only.
